# BookNowTech Payment Terms

**Version:** payments-v2

**Effective date:** September 1, 2026

These Payment Terms apply when you pay for a booking through BookNowTech. Please read them before you confirm your payment. When you check the acceptance box at checkout, you are agreeing to this exact version of this document.

BookNowTech is operated by Mobile Up Tech Inc. ("BookNowTech", "we", "us").

---

## 1\. Who you are paying

You are booking a service from an independent business (the "Business") that uses BookNowTech to accept online bookings. The Business \- not BookNowTech \- provides the service, sets its price, and is the merchant of record for your payment.

BookNowTech provides the booking and payment technology. We are not a party to your service agreement with the Business, and we do not deliver, supervise, or guarantee the service you book.

Your payment is processed directly to the Business's own account with our payment processor, Stripe. Because of this, the charge on your card or bank statement may appear under the Business's name rather than under "BookNowTech."

## 2\. What you are charged when you book

Before you enter your card details, the checkout screen shows you a full breakdown. Depending on how the Business has configured the service, you will be asked to pay either:

- **the full service price**, or  
- **a fixed deposit** \- a set dollar amount that is less than the full service price, or  
- **nothing at all** \- some services are booked online without any payment. If no payment is required, these Payment Terms do not apply to that booking and no booking fee is charged.

Where payment is required, the amount charged to your card is:

> **Total charged today \= (full service price *or* fixed deposit) \+ Online booking fee**

The exact figures for your booking are displayed on the checkout screen before you confirm. The amount shown is the amount charged. Nothing is added afterward.

## 3\. The Online booking fee

The Online booking fee is a fixed dollar amount charged by Mobile Up Tech Inc. for use of the online booking service. It is:

- a flat amount, never a percentage of your service price;  
- set by BookNowTech rather than by the Business, and it may differ from one booking site to another;  
- shown to you separately in the checkout breakdown before you pay; and  
- charged only when you make a payment online. If a service requires no online payment, there is no booking fee.

The booking fee that applies to your booking is the one displayed at the moment you check out. If BookNowTech changes its booking fee later, that change does not affect a booking you have already paid for.

## 4\. Deposits and the remaining balance

If the Business asks for a deposit, the deposit is a partial payment toward the service price. The rest of the service price \- the "remaining balance" \- is **not** collected by BookNowTech and is **not** charged to your card.

You arrange the remaining balance directly with the Business, on its own terms and by whatever method it accepts. BookNowTech shows the remaining balance to you for information only. We do not invoice it, track it, collect it, hold it, guarantee it, or record whether you have paid it.

The Online booking fee is not part of the service price and is not credited against your remaining balance.

## 5\. Payment authorization

By confirming payment, you authorize a single immediate charge to the card you provide, for the total amount displayed at checkout, in **US dollars**.

- **We charge immediately.** We do not place a temporary hold and charge you later.  
- **Cards only.** We accept credit and debit cards. We do not accept bank transfers, delayed-payment methods, digital wallets, or cash through the online booking flow.  
- **US dollars only.** All amounts are in USD. If your card is issued outside the United States, your bank may apply its own conversion or foreign transaction fees. Any such fees are set by your bank and are not charged, received, or controlled by BookNowTech or the Business.  
- **One booking, one charge.** Refreshing the page, resubmitting, or retrying a declined card will not create a second booking or a second charge.

Your card details are entered directly into our payment processor's secure fields. BookNowTech does not receive or store your full card number, security code, or other payment credentials.

## 6\. Your 15-minute hold

When you begin a paid booking, we hold your requested time slot for **15 minutes** while you complete payment. During that window:

- the slot is reserved and not offered to anyone else;  
- if your card is declined, you can usually correct the details or use a different card and try again without losing the slot \- though some card failures end the attempt outright, which releases the slot immediately and requires you to start a new booking; and  
- your booking is **not yet confirmed**.

If the 15 minutes pass without a successful payment, the hold expires and the slot is released. In the ordinary course no payment is taken. In the rare case that a payment reaches your card at or after the moment the hold expires, Section 13 applies.

## 7\. When your booking is actually confirmed

**A successful card charge is not by itself a confirmed booking.** Your booking is confirmed only when BookNowTech records it as scheduled and sends you a booking confirmation.

Please do not treat a payment success screen, a bank notification, or a pending charge as confirmation. Wait for the booking confirmation.

If your booking details change materially between the time you start checkout and the time you pay \- for example, the price, the deposit amount, the staff member, or the date or time \- we will try to stop the payment rather than charge you for something different from what you reviewed, and we will ask you to start again from the current details. If a charge has already gone through by that point, Section 13 applies.

## 8\. Cancellations

Cancellation policies \- including notice periods and any cancellation charges \- are set by the Business, not by BookNowTech. The Business's policy governs your booking. Please review it before you book and contact the Business with any questions.

**Cancelling a booking does not automatically produce a refund.** Cancellation and refund are separate. If your booking is cancelled \- by you or by the Business \- any money already paid is returned only if a refund is actually issued under Section 9\.

## 9\. Refunds

### 9.1 Money paid to the Business

Any refund of the service price or deposit you paid is at the **Business's** discretion, under the Business's own cancellation and refund policy. The Business issues such refunds itself, through its own account with our payment processor.

BookNowTech does not issue, approve, deny, or advance refunds of money paid to the Business, and cannot compel the Business to issue one. Direct all refund requests for the service price or deposit to the Business.

Two things to be aware of:

- **A refund does not cancel your appointment.** If the Business refunds you but neither of you cancels the booking, the appointment remains on the schedule. If you intend to cancel, cancel it as well.  
- **A refund from the Business does not return the Online booking fee.** The booking fee is handled separately under Section 9.2.

Where a refund is issued, it returns to the original card. The time it takes to appear on your statement is determined by your bank and the card networks, not by BookNowTech or the Business.

### 9.2 The Online booking fee

The booking fee is treated separately:

- **If you cancel**, the booking fee is **non-refundable**. It pays for the booking service, which was already provided.  
- **If the Business cancels** your booking, the booking fee is **refundable**.  
- **If BookNowTech cancels or unwinds** your booking, or your booking cannot be completed for a technical or operational reason on our side, the booking fee is **refundable**.

If you believe you are owed a booking fee refund, contact BookNowTech using the support details on the booking site.

### 9.3 Partial refunds

BookNowTech does not process partial refunds. A Business may choose to issue a partial refund directly through its own payment processor; that is a matter between you and the Business.

## 10\. Payment disputes and chargebacks

If something is wrong with a charge, please contact the Business first, and BookNowTech second. Most problems are resolved faster this way than through your bank.

Because the Business is the merchant of record, a formal dispute or chargeback you file with your bank is raised against the Business and is resolved between you, the Business, and the card networks. BookNowTech records the dispute and notifies the Business, but does not decide the outcome.

Nothing in these Payment Terms limits, waives, or delays any right you have under your cardholder agreement, the Fair Credit Billing Act, or other applicable law, including your right to dispute a charge with your card issuer.

## 11\. Receipts and confirmations

You will normally receive two separate emails:

1. **A payment receipt**, sent by our payment processor to the email address you gave at checkout. This is your receipt for the amount charged.  
2. **A booking confirmation**, sent by BookNowTech. This confirms your appointment and restates the service price, the amount you paid online, the Online booking fee, and any remaining balance.

The remaining balance shown on your confirmation is informational. It is not a bill from BookNowTech and is not evidence that you have or have not paid the Business.

BookNowTech does not send a second payment receipt. Please keep the processor's receipt for your records.

## 12\. Amounts we do not add

The total shown at checkout is complete. At checkout, BookNowTech does not calculate, add, collect, or remit:

- sales tax or any other tax;  
- gratuities or tips;  
- discounts, coupons, promotional codes, gift cards, or credits; or  
- card surcharges or convenience fees beyond the disclosed booking fee.

Card processing costs are borne by the Business. We do not pass them on to you as a separate line item or a higher total.

If tax or a tip applies to your service, the Business handles it directly with you, outside BookNowTech.

## 13\. If a payment goes through but your booking does not

Rarely, a payment may succeed while the booking itself does not complete. There are two situations, and they are handled differently.

**Your time slot is still held.** If the payment succeeded and we simply have not finished recording the booking, we keep the slot reserved for you \- including past the 15-minute window \- while we complete it. In most of these cases the booking finishes normally and you receive your confirmation.

**Your time slot has already been released.** If the hold had expired, or your booking details had already changed, the slot may have been given up before the payment landed. When that happens we will not create a booking you did not confirm, and we will not take the slot back from someone else. The payment is flagged for review instead.

In both situations, the payment is placed into a manual review queue and is not reversed automatically. We do not issue automatic refunds here, because in most cases the correct outcome is to complete the booking you paid for rather than cancel it.

**If you see a charge and do not receive a booking confirmation, contact BookNowTech using the support details on the booking site.** Include the date, the amount, and the Business you booked with. Your rights under Section 10 are unaffected.

## 15\. Records we keep

To operate payments securely we retain records of your booking and payment attempts, including attempts that failed, expired, or were abandoned before payment. We keep these for fraud prevention, duplicate-charge protection, accounting, and audit purposes.

An abandoned or failed attempt is not a booking and is not treated as one. Our Privacy Policy describes what we retain and for how long.
