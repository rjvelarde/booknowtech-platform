# BookNowTech Connect Terms

**Version:** connect-v1

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026

BookNowTech is operated by **Mobile Up Tech Inc.** ("BookNowTech", "we", "us").

By accepting these Connect Terms and proceeding with Stripe Connect onboarding, you agree to this exact version of this document.

## 1. BookNowTech and your Business

BookNowTech provides booking and payment technology that allows independent businesses to offer services, accept bookings, and, when enabled, accept online payments from their customers.

You remain the provider of the services offered through your BookNowTech booking site.

You control your services, pricing, availability, service delivery, cancellation policies, and customer relationships.

When payments are enabled for your Business, **you are the merchant of record for payments for your services.**

BookNowTech is not the provider of your underlying services and does not become a party to the service agreement between you and your customer.

## 2. Stripe Connect

BookNowTech uses Stripe Connect to provide payment-processing functionality.

To accept online payments through BookNowTech, you must establish and maintain an eligible Stripe connected account.

You authorize BookNowTech to initiate and facilitate the Stripe Connect onboarding process and to exchange information with Stripe as reasonably necessary to establish, operate, monitor, and support the payment functionality associated with your BookNowTech account.

Stripe provides the underlying payment-processing services.

BookNowTech is not a bank or financial institution and does not independently process card payments.

## 3. Your Stripe account information

You are responsible for providing Stripe with accurate, complete, and current information requested as part of onboarding or continued account verification.

This may include business, ownership, identity, banking, tax, and other information required by Stripe.

You are responsible for updating this information when necessary.

Stripe determines whether your connected account is eligible to process payments. BookNowTech does not guarantee that Stripe will approve your account or that your account will remain eligible to process payments.

## 4. Stripe agreements

Your use of Stripe and Stripe Connect is subject to the agreements, policies, restrictions, and requirements imposed by Stripe that apply to your connected account.

You are responsible for reviewing and complying with the Stripe agreements applicable to your Business.

These Connect Terms govern your use of BookNowTech's integration with Stripe Connect. They do not replace or modify agreements between you and Stripe.

## 5. Payment configuration

BookNowTech may allow payment requirements to be configured for services offered through your booking site.

Depending on the approved configuration for a service, a customer may be required to:

* pay the full service price online;
* pay a fixed deposit toward the service price; or
* make the booking without an online payment.

Where a deposit is collected, it is a partial payment toward your service price.

Any remaining service balance shown by BookNowTech is informational only. BookNowTech does not collect, invoice, track, hold, guarantee, or record payment of that remaining balance.

You are responsible for arranging collection of any remaining balance directly with your customer.

## 6. BookNowTech booking fee

BookNowTech may charge the customer a separately disclosed fixed-dollar booking fee when an online payment is made through BookNowTech.

The BookNowTech booking fee:

* is separate from your service price;
* is not part of a customer's deposit or payment to your Business;
* is not credited toward any remaining balance owed to your Business;
* is established by BookNowTech;
* is disclosed to the customer before payment; and
* may differ between BookNowTech booking sites or configurations.

You authorize BookNowTech and Stripe, as applicable, to collect and allocate the applicable BookNowTech booking fee through the Stripe Connect payment transaction.

A change to the BookNowTech booking fee does not retroactively change the fee associated with a payment already made.

## 7. Stripe processing costs

Stripe processing fees and other charges imposed by Stripe in connection with payments to your Business are your responsibility unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

BookNowTech does not add those processing costs to the customer's checkout total as a separate card-processing surcharge.

You are responsible for the services you offer through BookNowTech, including:

* service descriptions;

BookNowTech's provision of booking and payment technology does not transfer these responsibilities to BookNowTech.

## 9. Refunds of your service price or deposit

You control refunds of service-price or deposit amounts paid to your Business, subject to your policies, applicable Stripe requirements, card-network requirements, and applicable law.

You are responsible for issuing any approved refund of your service price or deposit through your own Stripe account.

BookNowTech does not approve, deny, advance, or fund refunds of amounts paid to your Business.

BookNowTech does not process partial refunds of your service price or deposit on your behalf.

Refunding a payment does not by itself cancel the associated BookNowTech appointment. If the appointment should also be cancelled, the booking must be cancelled separately.

## 10. BookNowTech booking-fee refunds

The BookNowTech booking fee is separate from money paid to your Business and is governed by BookNowTech's customer-facing Payment Terms.

Under the current Payment Terms:

* when the customer cancels, the BookNowTech booking fee is non-refundable;
* when the Business cancels the booking, the BookNowTech booking fee is refundable; and
* when BookNowTech cancels or unwinds the booking, or the booking cannot be completed because of a technical or operational reason on BookNowTech's side, the BookNowTech booking fee is refundable.

BookNowTech controls and administers refunds of the BookNowTech booking fee.

## 11. Disputes and chargebacks

Because you are the merchant of record, disputes and chargebacks concerning payments for your services are raised against your Business and are handled through your Stripe connected account.

You are responsible for responding to disputes, providing requested evidence, and paying amounts, fees, reversals, or other liabilities imposed in connection with your transactions, subject to Stripe's requirements and applicable law.

BookNowTech may record dispute information and provide notifications or technical information associated with the BookNowTech booking, but BookNowTech does not determine the outcome of a cardholder dispute or chargeback.

## 12. Connected-account readiness

Your ability to accept payments through BookNowTech depends upon your Stripe connected account remaining ready and eligible for payment processing.

BookNowTech may require, among other things, that:

* your Stripe account is properly connected to BookNowTech;
* charges are enabled;
* required card capabilities are active;
* required verification information has been provided;
* Stripe does not report outstanding requirements that prevent payment processing; and
* BookNowTech can verify sufficiently current account-readiness information.

You are responsible for responding to additional information or verification requests from Stripe.

## 13. Suspension or disabling of payment functionality

BookNowTech may disable, suspend, or refuse new payment execution when reasonably necessary, including when:

* your Stripe account is not ready or eligible;
* Stripe restricts or disables your account;
* required verification remains incomplete;
* BookNowTech detects a security, fraud, reconciliation, or operational risk;
* required payment configuration is incomplete or invalid;
* you violate applicable BookNowTech or Stripe requirements; or
* continued payment execution could create material risk for customers, your Business, BookNowTech, or the payment system.

Where technically and operationally appropriate, BookNowTech may disable payment execution without disabling unrelated booking functionality.

Disabling new payment execution does not necessarily stop processing required to safely resolve existing transactions.

## 14. Existing payment activity after suspension

If new payment execution is disabled, BookNowTech and Stripe may continue operations necessary to manage payment activity that occurred before the suspension.

These operations may include:

* webhook processing;
* transaction finalization;
* reconciliation;
* dispute and chargeback processing;
* refund-related processing;
* payment-status updates;
* audit and recordkeeping; and
* investigation or manual review of payment exceptions.

You remain responsible for obligations arising from transactions processed before payment functionality was suspended or terminated.

## 15. Payment and booking completion

A successful Stripe charge does not by itself guarantee that BookNowTech has completed the associated booking.

BookNowTech uses payment and booking state together to determine whether the booking has been successfully finalized.

In the uncommon event that payment succeeds but the booking cannot immediately be finalized, BookNowTech may place the transaction into reconciliation or manual review rather than automatically reversing the payment.

This process is intended to determine the correct outcome while preserving transaction and booking records.
